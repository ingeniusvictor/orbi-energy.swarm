# Future Systems

The following systems are officially declared as reserved modules or deferred expansions. They are documented here to keep the prototype baseline stable, compiles-clean, and clear of dead states.

## 1. Boss Encounters & Raid Targets
- **Titan Core / Quantum Titan**: Heavy capital guardian ships featuring multi-segment weak spots.
- **Worm Segments**: Boss-level serpentine enemies with independent segment physics and segment destruction rewards.

## 2. Advanced Cosmic Events
- **Whale Visual Event**: Passive space leviathans drifting across the background matrix.
- **Eclipse Visual Event**: Random global darkness that dampens solar weapon recharge rates while increasing parasite speed.

## 3. High-Fidelity Capture Tools
- **Photo Mode & Color Filters**: Capability to freeze the game and apply cinematic retro shader layers.

## 4. Advanced Swarm Synergies
- **Affinities & Elemental Fusions**: Merging custom Orbis (e.g. 3 Pulses or 3 Auras) into advanced fusion drones.
