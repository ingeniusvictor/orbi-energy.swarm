# ORBI ENERGY SWARM
## MODULE 0B.5 — MID-RUN UPGRADES

Mid-Run Upgrades (or retroactive tactical retrofits) are temporary, run-specific modifications unlocked during the intermission/break between sectors. They empower players to customize their builds dynamically, reacting to active wave modifiers and threats.

---

## 1. UPGRADE TYPE DEFINITION

Upgrades are grouped into five strategic categories to support distinct build archetypes:

- **OFFENSE (Rose)**: Increases elemental damage and fire rates.
- **DEFENSE (Cyan)**: Enhances deflector shields and core health.
- **SWARM (Amber)**: Speeds up recruitment, doubles splits, or expands size.
- **TACTICAL (Emerald)**: Maximizes formation bonuses and synchronizations.
- **FUSION (Violet)**: Integrates powerful combination effects for highly evolved units.

---

## 2. THE 12 IN-GAME UPGRADES

### 1. Solar Overcharge (`solar_overcharge`)
- **Category**: OFFENSE
- **Icon**: ☀️
- **Affinities**: Solar
- **Max Stacks**: 3
- **Description**: Increases Solar elemental damage by +25% per stack and enhances projectile glow density on screen.

### 2. Hydro Regenesis (`hydro_regenesis`)
- **Category**: DEFENSE
- **Icon**: 🌊
- **Affinities**: Hydro
- **Max Stacks**: 3
- **Description**: Restores +1 Shield every 4 seconds for each Hydro unit in your swarm. Stacks up to 3 times.

### 3. Wind Acceleration (`wind_acceleration`)
- **Category**: TACTICAL
- **Icon**: 💨
- **Affinities**: Wind
- **Max Stacks**: 3
- **Description**: Enhances Wind shooter attack velocity by +35% and increases overall bullet speed.

### 4. Thermal Expansion (`thermal_expansion`)
- **Category**: OFFENSE
- **Icon**: 🔥
- **Affinities**: Thermal
- **Max Stacks**: 3
- **Description**: Expands Thermal explosive splash damage radius by +40% on impact.

### 5. Quantum Stabilization (`quantum_stabilization`)
- **Category**: TACTICAL
- **Icon**: ⚛️
- **Affinities**: Quantum / General
- **Max Stacks**: 3
- **Description**: Increases FOTON's physical movement speed by +15% per stack.

### 6. Foton Integrity (`foton_integrity`)
- **Category**: DEFENSE
- **Icon**: 🛡️
- **Affinities**: General
- **Max Stacks**: 3
- **Description**: Reinforces FOTON's deflective shield grid, increasing maximum shield by +30 per stack.

### 7. Mitotic Mitosis (`mitotic_mitosis`)
- **Category**: SWARM
- **Icon**: 🧬
- **Affinities**: General
- **Max Stacks**: 2
- **Description**: Followers recruited have a 25% chance per stack to spawn an identical duplicate immediately.

### 8. Catalyst Accelerator (`catalyst_accelerator`)
- **Category**: OFFENSE
- **Icon**: ⚡
- **Affinities**: General
- **Max Stacks**: 3
- **Description**: Accelerates the base reload/fire rate of all swarm members by +15% per stack.

### 9. Formation Synchronizer (`formation_synchronizer`)
- **Category**: TACTICAL
- **Icon**: 📐
- **Affinities**: General
- **Max Stacks**: 3
- **Description**: Decreases tactical formation shift cooldowns by -30% per stack, allowing rapid combat repositioning.

### 10. Elemental Synthesis (`elemental_synthesis`)
- **Category**: FUSION
- **Icon**: 🧪
- **Affinities**: Multiple (Requires Solar & Hydro in Swarm)
- **Max Stacks**: 2
- **Description**: Unlocks dual elemental orbital plasma arcs between Solar and Hydro units.

### 11. Kinetic Feedback (`kinetic_feedback`)
- **Category**: OFFENSE
- **Icon**: 💫
- **Affinities**: Multiple (Requires Wind & Thermal in Swarm)
- **Max Stacks**: 2
- **Description**: Non-minion enemy destructions trigger a kinetic shockwave that repels nearby targets.

### 12. Swarm Overscale (`swarm_overscale`)
- **Category**: SWARM
- **Icon**: 🌌
- **Affinities**: General
- **Max Stacks**: 2
- **Description**: Increases total swarm follower size capacity by +6 per stack, exceeding standard constraints.
