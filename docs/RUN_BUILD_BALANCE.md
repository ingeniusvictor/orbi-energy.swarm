# ORBI ENERGY SWARM
## MODULE 0B.5 — RUN BUILD & BALANCE GUIDE

The addition of 12 temporary mid-run upgrades unlocks highly customizable, synergistic "Run Builds". This guide details the core archetypes, recommended formations, and optimal upgrade paths to survive up to the final boss.

---

## 1. CORE ARCHETYPES & SYNERGIES

### BUILD A: "THE SOLAR FIRESTORM" (Offensive Overload)
- **Primary Upgrades**: `solar_overcharge` (3), `thermal_expansion` (3), `catalyst_accelerator` (3)
- **Key Formation**: LINE / DELTA
- **How it works**: By stacking massive raw fire rate (+45% from Catalyst) and combining Solar elemental damage (+75%) with Thermal's expanded explosive splash radius (+120%), the swarm acts as an incinerating wall, drilling through high-density splitters and elites instantly.
- **Counter-measure**: Highly vulnerable to snipers. Maintain rapid kinetic movement.

### BUILD B: "THE HYDRO BASTION" (Unkillable Fortress)
- **Primary Upgrades**: `hydro_regenesis` (3), `foton_integrity` (3), `elemental_synthesis` (2)
- **Key Formation**: SHIELD
- **How it works**: By increasing max shields to +90 and stacking high-capacity passive shield regeneration (+1 shield every 4s per Hydro member), this build makes FOTON virtually immune to bullet spam. Adding Elemental Synthesis triggers dual-orbital plasma arcs that automatically clear close-range threats.
- **Counter-measure**: Lower raw DPS. Waves will take longer to complete; focus on collecting resources in the last few seconds of the wave.

### BUILD C: "THE WIND SKIRMISHER" (High-Speed Kiting)
- **Primary Upgrades**: `wind_acceleration` (3), `quantum_stabilization` (3), `formation_synchronizer` (3)
- **Key Formation**: CIRCLE / V_SHAPE
- **How it works**: This build turns FOTON into a rapid-recon interceptor (+45% player movement speed) and gives Wind projectile shooters extreme velocities (+105%). Formation Synchronizer reduces shifting cooldowns to virtually zero, allowing players to instantly transition from CIRCLE (collecting) to V_SHAPE (strafing).
- **Counter-measure**: Requires precise pointer steering. Avoid getting cornered near the world bounds.

### BUILD D: "THE OVERLORD SWARM" (Mitotic Overcrowding)
- **Primary Upgrades**: `mitotic_mitosis` (2), `swarm_overscale` (2), `kinetic_feedback` (2)
- **Key Formation**: SCATTERED
- **How it works**: Mitotic Mitosis gives every newly recruited follower a 50% chance to duplicate instantly. Swarm Overscale expands follower capacity by +12 (taking the cap up to 72 if base is maxed). Firing is highly automated and triggers chaotic kinetic shockwaves upon non-minion enemy destruction, pushing attackers away.
- **Counter-measure**: High visual density. Best enjoyed on MEDIUM or HIGH graphics presets to preserve rendering performance.
